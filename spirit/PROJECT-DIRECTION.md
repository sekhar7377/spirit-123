# SPIRIT — cinematic rebuild

## Client direction
Authentic Spirit title artwork and promotional assets; cinematic video-led opening; substantial immersive website; a coordinated team with explicit quality ownership. The initial typography-led portfolio treatment was rejected.

## Responsibilities
- Project lead / integration: root agent. Own creative choices, architecture, asset integration, delivery.
- Visual research specialist: assigned `visual_assets`; run stopped by account usage limit. Lead completes research.
- Creative / UX / story specialist: assigned `creative_ux`; diagnosis received, then stopped by account usage limit. Lead completes direction.
- Motion engineer: assigned `motion_engine`; run stopped by account usage limit. Lead completes implementation.
- QA: independent review pass by the lead while specialist slots are unavailable. Do not claim independent specialist QA occurred.

## Creative thesis
A film release experience built from the film's real campaign. Three materials: photographic flesh and sunlight; petrol-blue shadows and burgundy; white calligraphic title on black. Actual supplied film title artwork is the identity, never a replacement typeface. UI typography stays quiet.

## Journey
1. Short film-title motion and real asset loading; enter with sound or silently. Skip always available.
2. Full-screen first-look scene, authentic title, release date, primary Watch Sound Story action. Scene selector crossfades first look, character reveal and motion.
3. Film: a paced, cinematic scroll with sourced introduction and campaign milestones.
4. People: image-led cast selection and complete creative credits from the posters.
5. Theatre: locally available title motion, official T-Series YouTube Telugu and Hindi Sound Story links/embeds, clear fallback links.
6. Archive: full clean original posters, accessible fullscreen viewer and downloads of displayed campaign references.
7. Sound: supplied Telugu audio, true frequency-reactive visuals, transport, seek, volume, optional device-local file.

## Motion
Real WebGL atmosphere, inertial camera parallax, crossfade scenes, shutter transitions, logo reveal, scroll-driven image scale and text fade. Never rapid flash. Reduced motion stops automatic scene/camera movement. Mobile renders fewer effects.

## Authenticity
This is not studio-authorized. Keep a discreet, clear independent-concept credit. Do not invent trailers, plot, character names, quotes, or claim generated footage is official.
